# epilepsy-china-projection-2050
This study projects the future burden of idiopathic and secondary epilepsy across 31 provinces in China from 2024 to 2050. We used data from the Global Burden of Disease Study 2023 and applied a Bayesian Model Averaging (BMA) framework incorporating the Socio-demographic Index (SDI), age structure, and their interactions.


# Key features

- **Data source**: GBD 2023 (1990–2023)
- **Geographic scope**: 31 provinces of China
- **Outcomes**: Prevalence and Years Lived with Disability (YLDs)
- **Forecast horizon**: 2024–2050
- **Methodology**: Bayesian Model Averaging with SDI and age structure interactions
- **Validation**: Out-of-sample validation for model performance assessment
